The files in this directory contain the osculating end-state orbital elements 
for Plutinos that remain librating in the 3:2 mean-motion resonance with Neptune for 4~Gyr.  

Journal, authors, and date of publication will be updated after refereeing.

The osculating mean longitude of Neptune at the same timestep as all of these 
test particles is 60.157255251453343 degrees.  

StablePlutinos.txt contains osculating orbital elements, phi_32 libration centres 
and libration amplitudes for all of the non-Kozai Plutinos at the end of the 4Gyr simulation.

StableKozaiPlutinos.txt contains osculating orbital elements, phi_32 libration 
centres and libration amplitudes, and omega libration centres and libration 
amplitudes for all of the stable Kozai Plutinos at the end of the 4Gyr simulation.

KozaiFlippers.txt and UnstableKozai.txt contain the Plutinos which were in the 
Kozai resonance for part of the simulation.  Only osculating orbital elements 
are provided, since the libration amplitudes change throughout these simulations.  

Individual particle time series available upon request.
